## what is lynx combinator?

lynx combinator is socal's #1 ai program for youth — a 6-week bootcamp that turns young leaders into real builders. by the end of the program, every student ships 3 real ai products ready for their college portfolio.

this isn't a class about ai. it's a program where students *build with* it.

## what students build

every cohort produces three working products:

**ai chatbot** — a custom deployment that solves a real problem the student cares about.

**voice agent** — an interactive voice ai built for practice, coaching, or communication.

**customer support agent** — a fully integrated ai tool wired into the student's existing workflow or idea.

## live data & real outcomes

the program tracks real outcomes. a live dashboard pulls directly from google sheets, showing response volume by theme, customer fit, and the top fears young builders face when starting out.

this data shapes every cohort — so the program keeps improving with each class.

## the philosophy

> "most youth learn *about* ai in school. at lynx combinator, they *build with* it."

the gap between learning about technology and actually using it to create something is where most programs stop. lynx combinator starts there.

students leave with products, not just certificates.
